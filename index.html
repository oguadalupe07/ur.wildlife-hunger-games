THE TAKEAWAY

A sustainable campus is not one thing to every species.

A window, lawn, road, or construction site can be ordinary infrastructure to humans while changing food, shelter, movement, or collision risk for wildlife. Sustainability is partly about asking: who benefits, who is exposed, and who gets left out?
